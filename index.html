🚨 Major Concerns — Priority View
1
Zero Forbes Star Awards across the entire portfolio. None of the six properties currently hold a Forbes Star Rating — a significant gap given the portfolio's luxury/boutique positioning and competitive set.
2
No editorial features in Forbes destination guides. Despite Forbes covering all key markets (LA, Austin, SF, Miami Beach), none of the Proper properties appear in editorial "best hotel" recommendations — a missed visibility and credibility opportunity.
3
Competitor advantage growing. Competing luxury boutique hotels in these markets are actively pursuing and earning Forbes Star Awards and editorial placements, strengthening their positioning in AI search results and high-intent travel discovery.
4
AI/LLM citation impact. Forbes Travel Guide is a high-authority source frequently cited by AI search engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini). Absence from Forbes means missing citations in LLM-generated hotel recommendations — directly impacting AIO visibility.
📋 Recommended Dual-Track Engagement:

Track 1 — Standards & Ratings: Engage with Forbes Travel Guide's Star Award program. Properties undergo an anonymous inspection evaluating service standards, facilities, and guest experience. Earning a 4-Star or 5-Star rating provides a powerful credibility marker and drives visibility across Forbes' digital ecosystem and AI citations.

Track 2 — Editorial Outreach: Pursue editorial features in Forbes' destination guides for LA, Austin, SF, and Miami Beach. These "best hotels in [city]" articles are evergreen content that ranks highly in search and is frequently referenced by LLMs. Getting featured provides sustained organic visibility.

📈 Impact: Securing Forbes Star Awards and/or editorial placements would significantly strengthen the portfolio's positioning in both traditional search and AI-generated recommendations, creating a competitive moat that compounds over time.
